# Inventory and Publishers

## Types of inventory
Premium streaming apps sell ads inside their own shows and films. Free ad-supported channels stream linear-style channels with regular ad breaks. Device and platform owners sell ads on home screens and inside their own channels.

## Premium versus free channels
Premium apps usually deliver higher completion rates and higher CPMs. Free ad-supported channels deliver scale at lower CPMs but completion rates vary widely between channels.

## Private marketplaces
A private marketplace is an invitation-only auction run by a publisher for selected buyers. It offers better inventory than the open auction without the cost of a fully guaranteed deal.

## Supply path
Every intermediary between the advertiser and the publisher takes a fee. Shorter supply paths mean more of the spend reaches the publisher and less is lost in fees.

## Checking where ads ran
Ask for a placement report that lists each app or channel with impressions, completion rate and spend. A campaign that runs mostly on one publisher is exposed if that publisher has a bad week.
