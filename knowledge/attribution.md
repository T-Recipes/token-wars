# Attribution and Lift

## What attribution tries to answer
Attribution asks whether seeing the ad changed what a household did afterwards, such as visiting a website, downloading an app or buying in a store.

## Exposed versus control
The most reliable method compares households that saw the ad with similar households that did not. The difference in behaviour between the two groups is the lift.

## Why last-touch is misleading on TV
Last-touch attribution credits whatever the person clicked last. TV ads are never clicked, so last-touch models give them no credit even when they drove the visit.

## Reading lift results
A lift result needs a confidence range. A 4 percent lift with a range of minus 1 to plus 9 percent is not proof that the campaign worked.

## Moving budget
When moving budget between campaigns, prefer the campaign with the lowest cost per completed view and a positive lift result. Move budget in steps of no more than ten percent per quarter so the effect can be measured.
