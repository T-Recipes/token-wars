# Pricing and CPM

## CPM
CPM is the cost per thousand impressions. It is calculated as spend divided by impressions, multiplied by one thousand.

## Typical ranges
In this market, connected TV CPMs range from about $18 to $45. Premium streaming apps with live sports or first-run shows sit at the top of the range. Free ad-supported channels sit at the bottom.

## Cost per completed view
A low CPM is not always a bargain. Divide spend by completed views to get the cost per completed view. A campaign paying $20 CPM with a 70 percent completion rate pays more per finished ad than one paying $22 CPM with a 92 percent completion rate.

## When a CPM looks too high
A CPM more than 25 percent above the average for the same publisher type is worth questioning. Common causes are narrow audience targeting, short flights bought late, and premium placements that were not needed for the goal of the campaign.

## Negotiation levers
Longer commitments, flexible placement windows, and broader targeting all lower CPM. Guaranteed placements inside live events raise it.
