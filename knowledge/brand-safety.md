# Brand Safety and Suitability

## Safety versus suitability
Brand safety is about avoiding content that no advertiser should appear next to. Suitability is about avoiding content that is legal and acceptable but wrong for a particular brand, such as a family brand next to a late-night horror film.

## Blocklists
A blocklist names apps, channels or programme genres where the ad must never run. Blocklists should be short and reviewed every quarter. Long blocklists quietly remove good inventory and push prices up.

## Allowlists
An allowlist names the only places an ad may run. Allowlists give the most control but limit reach. They suit regulated categories such as financial services and alcohol.

## Content signals on CTV
Connected TV has fewer content signals than the web. Many apps pass only a genre and a rating. Where no signal is passed, treat the placement as unknown rather than safe.

## Reporting incidents
If an ad is reported next to unsuitable content, capture the app name, the time, and a photo of the screen. Pause the placement first and investigate second.
