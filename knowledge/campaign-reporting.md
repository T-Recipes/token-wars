# Campaign Reporting Standards

## The standard summary
Every campaign summary covers five numbers in this order: completion rate, households reached, frequency, CPM, and total spend. Completion rate comes first because finished views are the product being sold.

## Comparing campaigns
Only compare campaigns from the same quarter unless the reader asks for a trend. When ad lengths differ, compare average seconds watched as a share of ad length rather than raw seconds.

## Best and worst performers
Name the best performer by completion rate and the weakest by cost per completed view. Give one sentence of likely cause for each, based only on the data.

## Rounding
Round percentages to whole numbers, money to whole dollars, and frequency to one decimal place.

## What not to report
Do not report numbers from a partial week as if they were final. Do not report verification-partner numbers and publisher numbers side by side without saying which is which.

## Recommendations
A report may end with up to three recommendations. Each must name the campaign, the action, and the number that supports it.
