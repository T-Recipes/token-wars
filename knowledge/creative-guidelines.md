# Creative Guidelines

## Ad lengths
Connected TV ads are usually 15 or 30 seconds. Some publishers accept 6-second bumpers and 60-second long-form spots. Always check that the ad length matches the break length the publisher sells; a 30-second ad placed in a 15-second slot will be cut or rejected.

## The first three seconds
Show the brand or product within the first three seconds. Viewers decide very quickly whether to look at the screen or at their phone.

## Sound
Most connected TV ads are watched with sound on, unlike social video. Voice-over and music matter. Keep loudness within the publisher's limits or the ad will be turned down automatically.

## Calls to action
Viewers cannot click a TV ad. Use a short web address, a spoken brand name, or a QR code that stays on screen for at least five seconds.

## File specifications
Deliver high-bitrate files in the resolution the publisher requests. Low-quality files are re-encoded and can look noticeably worse on a large screen.
